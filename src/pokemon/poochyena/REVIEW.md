# Poochyena review log

Every clip, watched frame by frame from both sides (contact sheets at
`--density 2` and `3`, zoomed, then the GIFs at game resolution), and the
main ones in the compiled game's test battles. Reviewed against the
checklist: anticipation, follow-through, arcs with no sliding, effects from
the right emitter, a readable silhouette from our side (back view, the legs
under the text box) and the opponent's side, starts and ends on the stance.

From our side Poochyena shows its back and right flank, so every clip also
acts with what reads from behind: the head dives below the line of its back
or flies up above it, the hackles stand up along the back, and the tail (a
loose spring) whips up at each blow.
