# Blaziken review log

Every clip, watched frame by frame from both sides in the battle view (a contact sheet per clip and side: `build/tl/sheets2.mjs`, every 4th frame at game resolution; moves performed as the battle does, with their effects; clips that stay home cropped to the actor). Contact moves were measured against the advance-1 convention (the attacker's front stops 0.15 of its height short of the foe's front; Blaziken's front measures 0.425) with the calibrated home offsets zeroed, in a Blaziken mirror match: the gap is between the two bodies at the blow, in the foe's heights (`build/tl/reach.mjs`; Seismic Toss performed, the foe carried). Checked for each: anticipation, a snap into the blow, follow-through, arcs, the feet (a blow that drives the body in steps into it: the rear foot keeps its place while the front one steps, `stepIn` in the kit), the effect's emitter, a readable silhouette from our side (back view) and the foe's side, starts and ends on the stance.
